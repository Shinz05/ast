Our Mission

Wear quality clothes rightly made here in Rwanda, by Africans.

For 40 years, Albert Supply Textile has been at the forefront of making quality clothes in Rwanda. As the market grew and trends shifted, we opened another arm in China in 2003.

At the beginning of our establishment, we started as a household general merchandise company dealing in household products like food stuff and detergents, to name but a few. In 2019, Albert Supply Textile opened a clothes factory in Rwanda — and though the COVID-19 pandemic slowed our progress like it did for companies the world over, our resolve remained unwavering.

A Message From Our Founder

"It has taken me 22 years to reintroduce this brand in Rwanda again — however, our customers have always been with us. Today, we re-affirm our commitment to serve Rwandans by producing quality and affordable clothes, as an answer to the call the president made back in 2019."

"Give Rwandans quality clothes with the same standards you can find anywhere in the world, affordably — no need for Rwandans to wear second-hand clothes. And create employment for the Rwandan people, especially youth and women, to help bridge the skills gap."

"Join me as we journey together on this amazing textile road trip. Let's support Rwanda by wearing Rwandan products."

Albert Nsengiyumva
CEO & Founder

How We Work

From fibre to finish.

Sourcing

Selected Fibres

We work only with fibres that hold up to a designer's scrutiny — tested for weight, hand-feel, and consistency before a single meter is committed.

Weaving

Considered Construction

Every weave is chosen for how it will move on a body in motion, not just how it looks flat on a table.

Finishing

Final Inspection

Each roll is checked by hand before it reaches a design house — no shortcuts between our floor and the runway.
